Brand identityConcept project · 2026

Meridian Sleeper, an identity for a night train

A concept identity for an overnight rail service between Lisbon and Madrid, built around a setting moon that turns into the rails.

Case study
Brief
Night trains compete with budget flights on romance, not speed. The self set brief was an identity that sells the journey itself and works from a ticket stub up to a carriage side.
Approach
The mark is a low moon sliced by three rails that thicken toward the ground, so it reads as both a horizon and a track. A warm grotesque wordmark sits beside monospaced timetable type. Navy and gold carry the night, with coral reserved for wayfinding and alerts.
Outcome
Logo, colour and type system, route diagram language and a boarding pass. Next steps would be livery and station signage.
Role
Concept, identity design and art direction.
Tools
Vector artwork built in SVG.
UI/UXConcept project · 2026

Tend, a plant care app that asks for one tap a day

A mobile app concept that turns plant care into a single daily list instead of a wall of reminders.

Case study
Brief
Plant apps tend to fail by nagging: every plant sends its own notification and people mute them all. The brief was one calm daily moment that still keeps plants alive.
Approach
The home screen answers one question, which plants need water today. Each plant is a large touch target with its room as the secondary label, since people find plants by location. The detail screen leads with three facts and one primary action.
Outcome
Two key screens and a component set: list card, stat tile, history chart and primary button. Not yet tested with users, which would be the next step.
Role
Product concept, interface design.
Tools
Interface drawn in SVG.
Print and editorialConcept project · 2026

Slow Signal, a quarterly about radio and sound

Cover and opening spread for a 96 page independent magazine, designed for two colour risograph printing.

Case study
Brief
An independent title about sound has to make a silent medium feel audible, on a small press budget that allows only two inks.
Approach
Concentric arcs act as the masthead device and change position every issue, like a signal moving across the dial. One red and one black keep print costs down and give the series a spine colour. A high contrast serif for headlines sits on a strict two column grid.
Outcome
Cover system, feature opener template and a grid that scales from essays to photo stories.
Role
Editorial design, typography and cover art.
Tools
Layout drawn in SVG, set up for print production.
IllustrationConcept project · 2026

Forecast, four weathers from circles and bars

A geometric illustration series for a weather service, drawn with two shapes and a flat palette.

Case study
Brief
Weather icons are usually tiny and literal. The brief was a set that works as a large poster and as a small app tile without redrawing.
Approach
Every scene uses only circles and rounded bars. The moon is a circle with a bite taken by the sky colour, rain is the wind bar tilted, and the coral sun returns in the wind tile to tie the set together.
Outcome
Four scenes on a shared grid, with rules that extend to snow, fog and storm.
Role
Illustration and system design.
Tools
Vector illustration in SVG.
MotionConcept project · 2026

Echo Days, a looping ident for a music festival

A four second loop for screens and social posts. The animation above is the piece itself, playing live.

Case study
Brief
A festival ident has to loop for hours on stage screens without tiring the eye, and still read in two seconds on a phone.
Approach
Sound becomes rings that leave the wordmark at a steady tempo of one per second, in three colours that cycle. The type never moves, so the name is legible on every frame and the loop has no visible start or end.
Outcome
A seamless loop that scales to any aspect ratio, and a motion rule the rest of the campaign can reuse.
Role
Motion design and art direction.
Tools
SVG animated with CSS keyframes.
Brand identityConcept project · 2026

Kiln No. 4, a maker's mark for a ceramics studio

A small studio identity where the logo is a stamp that can be pressed into clay as well as printed.

Case study
Brief
A one person pottery sells at markets and online. The identity has to feel handmade without looking amateur, and cost almost nothing to apply.
Approach
The mark is a circular stamp sized to fit the base of a mug. Swing tags carry the facts buyers ask about, glaze number, firing temperature and batch size, set like a lab label. The palette comes from the glazes themselves.
Outcome
Stamp mark, swing tag system and glaze palette, all printable in one colour on kraft or cream stock.
Role
Identity design and packaging.
Tools
Vector artwork built in SVG.

About
